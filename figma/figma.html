Problem

The Challenge

Many college student portals have outdated interfaces that make it difficult for students to quickly access important academic information. Navigation is often cluttered, visual hierarchy is weak, and essential features require multiple clicks.

Objective

Goal

Design a modern student dashboard that improves usability through better navigation, cleaner layouts, stronger visual hierarchy, and quicker access to academic information.
